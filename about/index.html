About

Apparel for real groups and real dates

Texas Shirt Works prints custom apparel for community organizations, schools, businesses, events, and families.

We work with churches, booster clubs, local sports, offices that need uniforms or promo, organizers running a fundraiser or a show, and families planning holiday or reunion shirts. The job is practical: the right garments, a print people recognize, and a deadline that holds.

The studio is in Montgomery, Texas. When you write in, a person follows up about quantity, artwork, colors, and timing.

This site and the shop

txshirtworks.com is where custom work starts. Ready-made apparel is on shop.txshirtworks.com. The collection names match on both: Apparel, Community & Schools, Business & Promotional, and All Blank Apparel.

Custom orders are collected on this site. We confirm the artwork, garments, and timeline with you before anything is printed.

Who we print for

  • Community groups, schools, churches, and youth sports
  • Businesses ordering staff apparel, uniforms, or promotional pieces
  • Events, fundraisers, and concert or venue merch
  • Families ordering holiday, reunion, or gathering apparel
