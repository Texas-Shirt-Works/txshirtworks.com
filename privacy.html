Privacy policy

Effective July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Texas Shirt Works, a brand of Advisors Group International Inc ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use this website or engage our custom printed apparel.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, and the contents of messages you send through our contact form or other communications with us.
  • Service information — details reasonably necessary to deliver the services or products you request.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • provide the services or products you request;
  • respond to your inquiries;
  • keep you informed of the status of your project, order, or engagement, including shipping notifications where applicable;
  • request information that is missing or needed to complete your request; and
  • provide customer and client support.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate this website and deliver our services (for example, hosting and communications-delivery providers), and only as needed for those purposes, or where required by law.

Text messaging (SMS) program — Client Care Notifications

If you opt in to text messages from Texas Shirt Works, we operate the program Client Care Notifications. Messages may include responses to your inquiries, order confirmations, production and shipping updates, and requests for missing information. Message frequency varies based on your requests and activity. Message and data rates may apply.

Your consent to receive text messages, and the phone numbers and opt-in information collected for this program, are never shared with or sold to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Reply STOP at any time to cancel and stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help, or call or text us at (936) 297-6400, or use the contact form at https://txshirtworks.com/custom.

Data retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Please call or text us at (936) 297-6400, or use the contact form at https://txshirtworks.com/custom.
